Getting here

BART & bus

Downtown Berkeley BART is about a 15-minute walk (about ¾ mile).

AC Transit line 6 stops at Telegraph Ave & Haste St, about two minutes away. Lines 51B, 36, 22 and 27 stop on Durant Ave at Telegraph and at Dana, about three minutes away.

Parking

The city-owned Telegraph Channing Garage (2450 Durant Ave) is around the corner.

Metered street parking nearby takes coins, cards and the ParkMobile app.

Bike

A Bay Wheels station sits at Haste St & Telegraph Ave, about two minutes away, and there are public bike racks along Telegraph.
