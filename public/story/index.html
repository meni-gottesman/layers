Our Story

The Layers Story

01

Founder’s Story

Layers began with Nanno’s personal journey of learning what health really means.

What started as an interest in fitness gradually became a deeper understanding of how food, movement, sleep, recovery, and everyday habits work together. Living in New York introduced her to a broader wellness culture, but also made her question what “healthy” really meant when products could look healthy while still containing ingredients she would not choose for herself.

During the pandemic, Nanno spent more time studying nutrition, movement, ingredients, and recovery. She began experimenting with whole fruits, natural sweetness, texture, fiber, and function, learning how food could be both nourishing and genuinely enjoyable.

That curiosity became Layers.

The first Layers location opened in 2022. In 2024, the idea for Berkeley began, giving Nanno the opportunity to rethink and refine the recipes, menu, space, service, team, and overall experience. After more than a year of careful development, Layers Berkeley officially opened its doors in October 2025.

Today, Layers continues to grow with the same mindset it started with: stay curious, keep improving, and create something that helps people feel better in everyday life.

02

Why Layers Was Created

Layers was created from a simple belief: healthy food should feel honest.

We wanted smoothies to taste like real fruit, not fruit diluted with water or ice, or sweetness hidden behind syrups and processed sweeteners. What we say is in the cup should truly be what is in the cup.

We use whole ingredients for natural sweetness, flavor, texture, fiber, nourishment, and function. Every ingredient should have a reason to be there.

Layers was never created to be a diet brand or a place built around restriction. We believe in better ingredients, better choices, and better everyday habits without compromising taste or enjoyment.

If we say it is not there, it is not there.

03

Brand Philosophy

At Layers, we believe wellness is a whole system.

Food is one part of it, alongside movement, recovery, sleep, connection, and the habits that shape everyday life. That is why Layers is more than a place to eat.

We bring wellness into the Berkeley community through food, run clubs, Pilates, yoga, wellness events, and movement focused experiences that bring people together.

The same intention carries into everything we serve. We choose real ingredients, remove what does not need to be there, and create food that is both nourishing and enjoyable.

Our goal is to make wellness feel approachable, social, and part of everyday life.

Real food. Real movement. Real community.

04

Our Journey

Layers opened its first location in 2022, built through hands on learning, recipe development, customer conversations, and constant refinement.

In 2024, the vision for Berkeley began. Instead of simply recreating the original shop, we used the opportunity to evolve Layers by refining the products, curating the space, strengthening the team, and creating a more complete wellness experience.

After more than a year of testing, adjusting, and developing every detail, Layers Berkeley officially opened in October 2025.

Since then, the brand has continued to grow through its customers, team, university community, wellness partnerships, running events, Pilates, yoga, and local collaborations.

We are still learning, still refining, and still building Layers with the people around us.

05

How We Make It

At Layers, good food starts with intention.

We begin with whole ingredients and think carefully about what each one contributes, from flavor and texture to natural sweetness, fiber, nourishment, and function.

We use frozen fruit instead of ice, avoid added water, do not rely on refined sugar or syrups, and develop our bowl bases in house so we have greater control over ingredients, flavor, and texture.

Every recipe is tested and refined through real daily experience, including feedback from our team and customers.

Real ingredients. Intentional recipes. Nothing unnecessary.

  1. No ice

    Frozen fruit brings the cold without diluting the flavor.

  2. No water

    Full flavor and satisfying texture, without watering things down.

  3. No refined sugar or syrups

    Natural sweetness comes from whole ingredients.

  4. House-made bases

    Developed in-house for better control over ingredients, flavor, and texture.

  5. Functional ingredients

    Chosen with intention to support the purpose of each recipe.

Real Food. Functional Fuel.
