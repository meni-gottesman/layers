Our Story

A small Berkeley shop built around real food.

Layers is for people who want something clean, functional, and satisfying, without the usual shortcuts. We make our bases in house, blend without ice, skip added sugar, and build every item around ingredients with a purpose.

Class, work, the gym, or the middle of the day — Layers fits into real life.
