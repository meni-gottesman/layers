Staff tool

Prepare a photo

Makes a photo ready for the website editor: a JPEG no bigger than 2,400 pixels on its long side and under 3 MB, the most the editor accepts. It also removes hidden details stored in the photo, such as where it was taken.

Your photo stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere: the photo is resized right here in your browser.

JPEG, PNG, WebP, or an iPhone photo (HEIC)

This browser can’t open that photo

iPhone photos are often saved as HEIC, which Chrome and some other browsers can’t read. Two easy fixes:

  • Use Safari. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone, iPad or Mac and choose the photo again. Safari can read iPhone photos.
  • Save a JPEG copy first. On a Mac, open the photo in Preview, choose File → Export…, set Format to JPEG and click Save. Then choose that copy here.

To have your iPhone take JPEG photos from now on: Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible.
