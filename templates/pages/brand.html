Photos

  • Do Use daylight. Shoot near a window or outside, without flash. Daylight keeps the colours of the fruit and the shop true.
  • Do Show real people and the real product: customers, the team, the cups and bowls exactly as they’re served.
  • Do Shoot wide (landscape) for the wide bands across the page, and keep the subject near the middle, because the site trims the edges to fit.
  • Do Keep each photo under 3 MB. Run it through Prepare a photo before uploading it.
  • Don’t Add text, stickers, frames or filters. The site sets its own words over and around photos, so a plain photo works best.
